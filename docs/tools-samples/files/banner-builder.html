Maturity Model for Microsoft 365

Banner builder

Every level has its own color from the logo, and every competency has its own icon. A banner puts them together: the competency on the left, the level on the right. Build one below, copy or download it as SVG, and use it on a page, in a deck or on social media.

Build a banner

Pick a type, a competency and a level. How to elevate shows two levels, from and to. Practical scenarios and tools have no level. Banners are 1200 × 300 px.

Level profiles

The five colors of the logo are the five levels, in the order of the logo’s columns: purple, blue, orange, green, yellow. The staircase shows how high the level is. The keywords are the model’s own. Only purple carries white text; the other four carry dark text, so every label passes WCAG AA contrast.

Competency profiles

The icons are Microsoft’s Fluent UI System Icons (MIT License), the same visual language as Microsoft 365. Competencies have no color of their own: color always means level, so the two never get mixed up. Click a competency to put it in the banner.

The equalizer

On the model banner the staircase bounces like an equalizer for about three seconds and then settles into the staircase. As a loading indicator it loops only while something loads. People who have asked their system for reduced motion see a still staircase.
